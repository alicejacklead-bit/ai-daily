💡 Today's Take

🎯 Overnight, OpenRouter rewrote the prices of six major models all at once—the "shelf price" of model APIs is turning into a market rate that floats in real time with supply and demand. Aggregator platforms are no longer just channels; they are the actual holders of pricing power. The most noteworthy item today is not a model release but OpenRouter's price sheet: six models repriced on the same day, with flagships getting cheaper and small models getting more expensive, showing that the aggregation layer is already pricing on supply and demand rather than cost. Echoing this is the Muse negotiation—after Agents captured 51% of agentic order share, platforms were forced to sit down and negotiate terms, and bargaining power between channels and Agents is being redistributed. The funding picture shows a clear polarization: LangChain raised $300 million at a $3 billion valuation, while Anthropic's $900 billion debt commitment was priced at junk level, showing that capital is shifting from "betting on model capability" to "betting on the Agent engineering layer and the distribution layer." For platforms building video-generation APIs, the conclusion is: upstream price swings will be faster and more frequent, and the ability to lock in prices matters more than the level of unit prices.
