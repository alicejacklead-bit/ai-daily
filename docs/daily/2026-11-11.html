💡 Today's Take

🎯 Anthropic kicks off its IPO roadshow at $70–78 per share with a target valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, while Claude Fable 5.2 tops the model leaderboard—frontier labs are packaging "technical leadership" directly into public-market assets, and the model price war has revealed a new trick in Gemini 4 Carbon's "low price first, then double it" pricing. What's most worth remembering today isn't any single model, but the divergence in pricing strategy: Gemini 4 Carbon is using "low launch price, doubling on December 1" to grab migrations, MiniMax is using open weights plus a limited-time 50% discount to grab an ecosystem position, and on OpenRouter, DeepSeek price hikes and MiniMax price cuts are happening at the same time—price has become a tool for customer acquisition and positioning rather than a natural reflection of cost. Upstream capital is tightening in tandem: Anthropic is roadshowing at $70–78 per share with a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, effectively handing pricing power over frontier labs to the secondary market, so future model price hikes will more easily invite the question "on what grounds?" On the Agent side, two lines run in opposite directions: Adobe opening MCP lets Agents into professional creative workflows, while eBay blocking Agents reminds everyone that an Agent's capability boundary depends on whether the platform opens the door. For video/image API platforms, this means product selection logic must shift from "who's cheaper" to "who has been repeatedly validated by channels on multi-turn editing and stability."

💰 Opportunities

  • Build a cost-routing service for the "December 1 pricing switch": Gemini 4 Carbon will double from $2/$10 to $4/$20 on December 1, while multiple DeepSeek and MiniMax lines on OpenRouter change price in the same week. API platforms and small-to-mid-sized SaaS teams that need to recalculate model routing weekly lack a tool that can automatically compare prices and reroute traffic before and after the switch date—based on the Gemini 4 Carbon pricing announcement and changes in the OpenRouter model table.
  • Provide creative teams with an "Agent-callable" image/video generation middleware layer: Adobe opening MCP lets third-party Agents access and modify Creative Cloud content, meaning generative models need to expose interfaces in an Agent-friendly way (stable multi-turn editing, RGBA transparent output, rollback capability).
