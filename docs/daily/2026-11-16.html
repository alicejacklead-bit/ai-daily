💡 Today's Take

🎯 Six major models on OpenRouter changed prices on the same day, with a mix of increases and decreases—the "shelf price" of model APIs has shifted from quarterly-level fluctuations to weekly-level fluctuations, and aggregation platforms are becoming the real price-discovery mechanism for model pricing. Today's price signals are more worth reading than the model signals: on OpenRouter, the DeepSeek family saw major price cuts, OpenAI and Qwen small models went up in price, and batch output prices rose in the opposite direction—showing that aggregator platforms have already repriced by "task tier" rather than raising or lowering everything across the board. On the supply side, two forks are emerging at once—Meituan ran a 1.6-trillion-parameter Agent model on Ascend, while Anthropic sidesteps pixel generation by "writing code to paint"—both proving that frontier capabilities need not follow the same compute and architecture path. On the demand side, WaveSpeedAI's featured slot has shifted from image back to video, and Replicate has cut its lowest-priced CPU tier—channels are reallocating traffic and gross margin. For video model API platforms, the price-comparison window is compressing from quarterly to weekly; shelf placement and tier design are themselves competitiveness.
