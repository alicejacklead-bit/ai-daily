💡 Today's Take

🎯 fal's valuation doubled to $16 billion in half a year, and OpenRouter's flagship models raised prices across the board — the "water sellers" of generative media are simultaneously harvesting demand dividends and pricing power, and the pressure on the middle layer that purely resells model APIs will only grow. The most notable combined signal today is this: the distribution layer is raising prices, raising funding, and fighting for entry points. fal's valuation doubled, Replicate is using a 50% discount to drive traffic, and Volcano Ark has expanded its integration tool list to more than ten Agent clients. These three things point to the same judgment—the competitive focus of generative media models is shifting from "model capability" to "who controls developers' invocation entry points." At the same time, Anthropic has built incremental context transmission into its API, and LangSmith is pushing Agent tracing into customers' own cloud accounts, showing that Agent infrastructure is sinking simultaneously in two directions: "cheaper" and "more auditable." For platforms building video model APIs, the most dangerous position is the pure resale layer that has neither a model generation gap nor a distribution entry point.
