💡 Today's Take

🎯 On OpenRouter, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's input price has been cut to 1/25 of the original, and the model API price war is shifting from "price cuts" to "repricing," which will reshuffle the gross-margin structure of the aggregation layer. Today's strongest signal is a "double de-bubbling of price and capability": on OpenRouter, Flash-tier models are being pushed to near-electricity pricing, meaning model capability itself is rapidly commoditizing; at the same time, Co-Design Bench punctures the old 99% benchmark with a real pass rate of 26.4%, showing that what's commoditizing is "looks like it can do it," not "can actually deliver." The capital side is converging in the same direction—OpenAI's valuation below initial expectations, Anthropic's IPO valuation revised down repeatedly, world models abandoned by capital—money is shifting from "model narratives" to harder links like "power, distribution, process constraints." Progress on the Agent side confirms this too: today's hottest topics aren't new models, but engineering scaffolding like browser runtimes, spec-first review gates, and Skills repositories.
