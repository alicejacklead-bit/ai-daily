### Today's Take
The clearest signal today is that the "price anchor" for model APIs has been re-pegged: Astra and Fable 5.2 both sit at $8/$40, while Sol is pushed down to $2/$10 — frontier capability is being commoditized at roughly 80% of the previous generation's price. At the same time, Perplexity has turned cross-checking among 16 heterogeneous Agents into a paid tier, while the academic side has just shown that most of the gains from multi-Agent setups come from compute rather than coordination — supply is getting cheaper, consumption is getting more expensive, and margins in the middle layer are being squeezed from both ends. Aggregation platforms and regional sovereign inference (Sarvam) are attacking the token-resale business from both sides, and the room for pure "forwarding" is rapidly disappearing. For the video generation track, the cost collapse on the text side means multimodal workflows and the creative experience are where the money can actually be made.
