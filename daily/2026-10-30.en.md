### Today's Take

The clearest signal today is that "the Agent's outer layer is replacing the model itself as the focus of competition": Hermit trains the harness's external layer, claude-mem builds cross-session memory, and OpenBot makes auditing a default capability—three things pointing to the same judgment: the marginal gains from swapping models are declining, while the combination of memory, skills, and auditability is determining product experience. The second signal is that platforms are swallowing the application layer upward: Gemini edits Docs/Sheets/PDF directly in chat, squeezing the room for pure document-generation tools, whose value must move up to "cross-format batch processing and enterprise governance." Third, costs are loosening in three directions at once—OpenAI doubles usage and hands out API credits, AssemblyAI pushes TTS down to $15 per million characters, and joey-1 uses a 2B model to handle routing decisions at $0.01 per million tokens; the margin models for voice and decision-making Agents are worth recalculating today. Finally, Anthropic's study showing that experts adopt an Agent's random opinions as their own is a lesson every team building decision-making Agents should read: defaults are product power.

### Opportunities

- **"Agent asset inventory and behavior auditing" for government and enterprises**: Based on WIRED's report that Accenture's personal Claude Agent ran on Pentagon networks for two months before being discovered, plus OpenAI's layered monitoring approach for detecting frontier-model misalignment. Buyers with Agent admission requirements (defense, finance, healthcare) now lack not model capability but visibility into "who is running what Agent and what it did"—compliance products like this sell better than model capability.
- **Cost restructuring and outsourcing for voice Agents**: Based on AssemblyAI TTS-1 at $15 per million characters with unlimited concurrency and no contract, plus the voice-stack consolidation from Deepgram's acquisition of Daily. You can now build outbound-call, booking, and follow-up voice agents for small and mid-sized merchants at extremely low marginal cost, taking a share of call volume instead of charging per seat.
- **Bidding privatized models into the European compliance market**: Based on Mistral's open-sourcing of the 1T Le Chonk (trained entirely in Europe) and the hosting uncertainty from the DOJ's review of Nvidia's acquisition of Hugging Face. For European clients with hard data-residency requirements, you can package "open weights + local deployment + mirrored backup" and turn compliance itself into a premium.

### Worth Exploring

- **Personally reproduce Anthropic's "random-preference Agent" experiment**: Use 20-30 internal colleagues as a small sample, let a coin flip decide the Agent's preferences, and measure their follow rate on judgment questions.
