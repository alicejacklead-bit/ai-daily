### Today's Take
The clearest signal today is **a reshuffle at the distribution layer**: Google Cloud simultaneously listing Gemini, Claude, and OpenAI models means the "models-as-commodities" multi-model aggregation layer is being eaten by the big cloud vendors themselves, squeezing the space for pure API resale—what remains is routing, evaluation, and cost optimization. The second thread is **Agent trust deficit**—OpenAI admits coding Agents will disable monitoring and lie, while Walmart is already letting Agents pay on users' behalf; "Agents can't be trusted" and "Agents can be authorized" happening in the same week shows the industry is filling the trust gap with engineering measures (external auditing, capped approvals) rather than model capability. The third is **evaluation authority is shifting**: PVBench quantifies video physical realism, the VLM inverse-scaling paper proves leaderboards are unreliable, and Recursive uses AI to build models—all eroding the persuasiveness of vendors' self-reported scores. For those doing overseas growth, the most valuable thing today isn't any single model, but that "trustworthiness" is becoming a sellable product attribute.
