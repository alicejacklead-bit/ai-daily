### Today's Take
The clearest signal today is that "the Harness layer is becoming its own layer": Factory Droid 1.0 emphasizes model-agnosticism, Vercel's agent-desktop replaces pixels with accessibility trees, and EasyProver approaches benchmark saturation through Agent orchestration rather than training—capability gains increasingly come from the shell rather than the base. Echoing this is tightening on the supply side: Anthropic expands watermarking while leaving loopholes, bans accounts that "touch training larger models," and the "usage compliance risk" of model APIs becomes a selection variable for the first time. On the funding side, OpenAI raised only one-third of its target, meaning the sustainability of subsidies and price wars will be discounted, while Agent Sandbox's tens of millions of daily creations show demand is still accelerating. In one sentence: demand is rising, supply is tightening, capital is receding, and engineering capability in the middle layer is the most certain arbitrage space.

Build third-party evaluations that provide cost-success-rate curves by task type and sell them to enterprises selecting Agents. Basis: Factory Droid 1.0 and Terminal-Bench 4 data.

### Worth Exploring
- **Run Glance's shared prefill reranking yourself**: Merge the reranker into the 9B generator on an existing RAG pipeline, measure end-to-end latency and memory usage, and focus on verifying the scoring stability of the pointer head under long contexts.
