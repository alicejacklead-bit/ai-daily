### Today's Take

What is most worth remembering today is not any particular model, but the collective shift in pricing logic: OpenAI is converting subscriptions to token quotas while simultaneously cracking down on multi-account arbitrage—essentially an admission that "unlimited" is unsustainable in the face of inference costs. At the same time, on the funding side, the round shrank from 30 billion to 11 billion, and Meta has turned to hundred-billion-scale debt financing, showing that the upstream subsidies supporting low-cost APIs are receding. Together, these two developments mean that over the next 6–12 months, API and consumer subscription prices are highly likely to only go up, not down. On the technical side, the trend is toward "architectural efficiency"—DeepSeek's Router-Worker halves p95, and LangChain adds browser operation, both squeezing out capacity without changing models. For categories with high per-generation costs such as video generation, whoever first gets a "base quota + pay-as-you-go overage" package design working will be first to capture gross margin.
