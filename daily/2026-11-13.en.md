### Today's Take
Today's signals point to the same thing: both compute and distribution are loosening at the same time. The leadership vacuum at Nvidia turns both GPU supply and the Hugging Face acquisition into variables, while the price sheets from OpenRouter and Volcano Ark show on the same day that subsidies for low-cost inference are receding, and that the video and image side is beginning to re-tier with "per-token + minimum spend" and "open-source low-cost models." For video generation API platforms, the real competitive point is shifting from "whether you can call the strongest model" to "whether you can orchestrate expensive and cheap models into the same workflow," and the free output of the Gemini Decisions API lowers the cost structure of this route by another notch. It is reasonable to expect a wave of hybrid products in the coming weeks that "use decision models for judgment and video models for generation."
